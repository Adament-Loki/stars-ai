# Growth 50-turn playtest vs baseline

Same seed (`playtests/seed/5Comps11Total`), seats 1–4 on Balanced, 50 hosted turns, year 2450. The growth run is `playtests/runs/growth-50turn` (allied seats 1–4, expansion and cheap allied-border starbases). It finished 50/50 in about 9.2 minutes.

The baseline is the earlier non-allied 50-turn host. The allied run is the 50-turn game after alliances were added and before this expansion pass. Host battles diverge, so this is one sample of each build.

Observer population is the decoded census figure, not the journal colonist count. Observer score is our composite, not the Stars! built-in score. Fleet mass still decodes as 0.

## What changed during the game

At year 2425 the growth seats were ahead on territory: 24 planets against 18 in the baseline and 17 in the allied run. Population was about the same (15,346 vs 15,530). The land grab showed up as more, thinner colonies.

From year 2425 to 2450 the baseline added 48 team planets. The growth run added 30 and finished 12 planets behind the baseline. P1 is most of that miss: 10 colonizations and 11 planets, against 17 colonizations and 18 planets before. At the final turn P1 and P3 were scouting, while P2 and P4 were still on expand.

P3 is the one seat that grew harder on the worlds it kept: population 16,839 vs 15,526 and factories 1,292 vs 1,030, on 14 planets instead of 19. P2 kept a similar planet count but lost about 6,200 population, and P5 captured planet 100 from P2. P4’s factory count fell from 552 to 360.

Starbase count for the team is 9, the same as the baseline. Cheaper border stations did not produce a larger network. P1 finished with 3 bases instead of 2.

P6 lost control of 11 planets in this run (3 in the baseline). Those worlds did not move to seats 1–4. P6 still finished with more population and more ships than in the baseline.

## Reading

The first 25 turns expanded faster. The second 25 turns did not turn that into a larger empire, and the team finished smaller and less populated than the baseline. One hosted sample cannot separate map luck from the policy, but the late stall is large enough that the current expansion package is not yet a gain on this seed.
