# Midgame 50-turn playtest

Same seed, seats 1–4 allied and Balanced, 50 hosted turns, year 2450. Output is `playtests/runs/midgame-50turn`. The baseline is the original non-allied 50-turn host. The growth run is the previous allied game before this phase change.

Player 1 finished at 11 planets and 7,899 population, against 18 and 12,307 in the baseline and 11 and 7,596 in the growth run. At the last turn Players 1–3 were in `midgame` with develop as the primary objective. Player 4 was still in `frontier_contact` and still expanding, at 10 planets.

P6 led by 65.0% over second place. Second place was still P5. P5 captured planet 100 from Player 2.

## What the phase change did

The hard switch happened. It happened while the empires were still small, because a saturated explored map also enters midgame, and allied surveys make a lot of the map look explored. Develop then outranked expand, so the late game built up the worlds already held instead of adding the colonies the baseline added after year 2425.
